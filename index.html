ABOUT US

A House Built
on Prayer.

Welcome to Christ Apostolic Church Agbala Adura — the Palace of Prayer.

We are a community of believers committed to worshipping God, growing in faith, standing together in prayer and sharing the love of Christ. At Agbala Adura, we believe that the church is more than a place of worship — it is a spiritual family where people are encouraged, relationships are strengthened and lives are shaped by the Word of God.

Located in Mushin, Lagos, Christ Apostolic Church Agbala Adura — Palace of Prayer continues the spiritual legacy established by its founders, Late Prophet and Lady Evangelist Aboluwarin. Their devotion to prayer, faith and service remains an important part of the foundation upon which the church continues to grow.

Through worship services, prayer meetings, Bible teaching, fellowship and community activities, we seek to create an atmosphere where individuals and families can encounter God, receive encouragement and grow together in faith.

We remain committed to being a praying church, a loving family and a place where people can discover and live out their purpose in Christ.

OUR FOUNDATION

A legacy of faith.

The spiritual foundation of Agbala Adura was laid through the ministry of its co-founders.

CO-FOUNDER

Late Prophet Aboluwarin

Late Prophet Aboluwarin was a co-founder of Christ Apostolic Church Agbala Adura. Together with his wife, Lady Evangelist Aboluwarin, he helped establish the spiritual foundation of the church and its enduring commitment to prayer, faith and the Word of God.

CO-FOUNDER

Lady Evangelist Aboluwarin

Lady Evangelist Aboluwarin was a co-founder of Christ Apostolic Church Agbala Adura. Together with her husband, Late Prophet Aboluwarin, she helped lay the spiritual foundation of the church and contributed to its growth through a life devoted to faith, prayer and service.

DISTRICT SUPERINTENDENT IN CHARGE

At Agbala Adura — Palace of Prayer, we are committed to building a church family where prayer, worship, fellowship and the teaching of God's Word remain central.
